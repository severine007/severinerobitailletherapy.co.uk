What I can help with

What I can help with

Whether you're dealing with something specific or just know that something needs to change, therapy can offer a space to explore what's going on beneath the surface.

You don't have to have a diagnosis to come to therapy. You also don't need to know what the issue is — it could just be a feeling that something is not quite right, or a wish to understand yourself and others better.

Below are some of the issues I have experience working with, though the list isn't exhaustive. If you're unsure whether I can help, please do get in touch.

General difficulties

Anxiety and panic
Depression and low mood
Stress and overwhelm
Loneliness and isolation
Low self-esteem
Relationship difficulties
Family issues and conflict
Bereavement and loss
Life transitions and major changes
Career transitions

Identity, trauma & complexity

Gender identity and exploration
Sexuality and sexual identity
Cultural identity and belonging
Family expectations vs. personal identity
Trauma (childhood, complex)
Abuse (physical, emotional, sexual) and bullying
ADHD / neurodivergent experiences
Performance and creative pressures
Suicidal thoughts

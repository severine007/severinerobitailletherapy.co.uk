Fees & Practicalities

Fees & Practicalities

Everything you need to know about sessions, location and payment.

Session length and fee

Sessions are weekly and last 50 minutes. They are on the same day and at the same time each week.

My fee is £80 a session although I am able to offer a reduced rate in certain circumstances — please feel free to enquire at contact@severinerobitailletherapy.co.uk

Payment

My preferred payment method is bank transfer.

Consultation sessions (our first 2 meetings) are payable on the day. After that, I send an invoice at the end of each month, payable within one week. Weekly invoices can be arranged if you prefer.

I am currently not able to accept clients through insurance companies.

Practice info

I currently work in person on Mondays in Central London, near King's Cross. I also have occasional availability in London Bridge. If you are looking for a different day or location, please do get in touch.

Relational Spaces (King's Cross Business Centre)
Room 202, 180–186 King's Cross Rd
London, WC1X 9DE

There is a lift in the building, making the room accessible to wheelchair users and those with reduced mobility.

Online / phone sessions

Online or phone sessions are available Tuesday – Friday.

I currently work online via Google Meet or WhatsApp call. We agree on our method of contact in our first meetings.

For online or phone sessions it is important that you're in a safe, private space where you won't be overheard so you can speak freely.
